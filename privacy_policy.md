**Privacy Policy for Pantry Expiry Board**
Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pantry Expiry Board handles information when you use the app.

Pantry Expiry Board is provided by Antonio Gutierrez Sanchez. You can contact us at antogs99@gmail.com.

1. Summary
Pantry Expiry Board is designed to be local-first. Your pantry item names, quantities, zones, best-by dates, notes, and history are stored on your device.

The app does not require an account. The app does not sell your personal information. The app does not show ads.

The app uses Firebase/Google Analytics to understand general app usage and improve the product.

2. Information You Enter
You may enter information such as:

Pantry item names
Quantities and units
Storage zones
Best-by dates
Reminder preferences
Notes
Consumed item history
This information is stored locally on your device. It is not intentionally uploaded to our own server.

Because this information stays on your device, you are responsible for managing your device, backups, and app data.

3. Analytics Information
Pantry Expiry Board uses Firebase/Google Analytics to collect usage and diagnostic information about how the app is used.

This may include information such as:

App launches and screen views
App interactions, such as adding an item or opening the paywall
App version
Device and operating system information
Approximate technical identifiers, such as an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase
General performance and diagnostic information
Analytics events are used to understand app usage, improve features, fix issues, and make product decisions.

The app does not intentionally send pantry item names, notes, exact food dates, or pantry contents as analytics event parameters.

4. Purchase Information
If you make an in-app purchase, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store.

We may receive limited purchase status information from Apple or StoreKit, such as whether a purchase or restore succeeded, so the app can unlock paid features.

We do not receive your full payment card details.

5. Notifications
If you enable reminders, Pantry Expiry Board may schedule local notifications on your device.

These reminders are handled locally through iOS notification services. Pantry Expiry Board does not use a server to send push notifications.

You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

6. How Information Is Used
Information is used to:

Provide the app’s pantry tracking features
Save and display your items, dates, zones, and history
Schedule local reminders
Provide paid feature access
Understand general app usage through analytics
Improve app performance, usability, and reliability
Respond to support requests
7. Sharing Of Information
We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used by the app, including:

Apple, for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, and device-level services
Google/Firebase, for analytics and app measurement
These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Retention
Pantry data you enter is stored on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

Analytics data is retained according to Firebase/Google Analytics settings and policies.

Support emails you send may be retained as needed to respond to your request and maintain records.

9. Deleting Your Data
You can delete pantry data inside the app where deletion controls are available. You can also delete the app from your device to remove locally stored app data, subject to any iOS backups or device-level retention.

For analytics data associated with Firebase/Google Analytics, contact antogs99@gmail.com and we will try to assist where reasonably possible, though some analytics data may not be directly identifiable to you.

10. Children’s Privacy
Pantry Expiry Board is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact antogs99@gmail.com.

11. International Users
If you use the app outside the United States, your information may be processed in countries where Apple, Google, Firebase, or other service providers operate.

12. Security
We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no app, device, or network can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because pantry data is stored locally, protecting your device with a passcode, biometric authentication, and secure backups can help protect your information.

13. Changes To This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be identified by the “Last updated” date above.

Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
